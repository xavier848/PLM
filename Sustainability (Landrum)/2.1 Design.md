You've learned about sustainability and the negative impacts of human activities, particularly with regard to businesses and products. To change these negative environmental impacts of products, we begin with DESIGN.

There are no universally adopted design principles for sustainability, but we can look at the practices of many designers and companies and identify the following common principles (University of Illinois, 2020).

- use non-toxic, sustainably produced, or recycled materials
- use products and processes that are energy efficient
- create longer-lasting more durable products
- design for reuse and recycling
- follow sustainable design standards
- use life cycle analysis to consider impacts across the entire product life cycle
- shift from product ownership to service provision
- use locally-sourced, sustainable, and renewable materials

**Principles of Sustainable Design**

1. Learn from nature - fit form to function, produce no waste, recycle everything
2. Look at life cycles - use renewable resources and evaluate environment impact of all materials and products throughout the life cycle
3. Respect for humans and the environment - healthy design is good for planet and people
4. Care of the local environment - a closer fit to the local environment is best
5. Consider the future - respect for the future (FibreGuard, 2021)
